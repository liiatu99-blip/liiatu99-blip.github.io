关于我About Me

你好，我是谢纪隆（Jiu），毕业于西安交通大学设计类专业，是一名专注于视觉表达与交互体验的设计师。我拥有丰富的行业与项目实践经验，擅长从用户需求、信息结构和品牌语境出发，将复杂内容转化为清晰、直观且富有情感连接的数字界面。在设计工作中，我重视结构、节奏与细节，持续探索功能性与审美表达之间的平衡；同时密切关注并主动学习前沿 AI Agent 技术，思考人工智能如何拓展设计流程、协作方式与产品体验的边界。I’m Jiu, a visual interaction designer with a design degree from Xi’an Jiaotong University and substantial experience across digital products and creative projects. I translate complex information into clear, intuitive interfaces by connecting user needs, information architecture, and brand context. My practice values structure, rhythm, and detail while balancing function with expressive aesthetics. I also actively study emerging AI agent technologies and explore how intelligent systems can expand design workflows, collaboration, and product experiences.
